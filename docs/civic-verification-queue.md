## Evidence status definitions

- `SUPPORTED` — an accessible official source directly states the claim.
- `PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED` — the source supports only some of the claim or scope.
- `HISTORICAL` — the claim is supported only by a date-limited or older source.
- `CONFLICTING` — official sources give materially different information.
- `UNCONFIRMED` — the cited official evidence does not establish the claim or
  current value.
- `NOT_APPLICABLE` — the field does not apply to this service, with reason.

## Human review workflow — actual local UI and limitations

No request has been submitted and no office has been contacted for this
checklist. The project implements local administration only; it does not
assign reviewers or send confirmation requests to government offices.

### Reviewer 1 — prepare and submit

1. Start the local API and Vite frontend as described in
   [admin-runbook.md](admin-runbook.md), then sign in at `/admin` with an
   existing `reviewer` or `admin` account. A `viewer` account is read-only.
2. An admin/coordinator must designate a real person to act as Reviewer 1
   outside the application. There is no in-app assignment field or assignment
   action. Use the records page
   `/admin/records?type=services&verification=unverified`, then open the
   relevant service record (IDs `101`, `104`, or `107`).
3. Ask the responsible office the questions above and retain the official
   notice or written response with its source, title, date, exact wording,
   fields supported, and unresolved items. Independently compare every
   catalog claim. Do not change a fact based on an unsupported or ambiguous
   response.
4. The record-detail edit form currently exposes only `title`, `description`,
   `category`, `sourceUrl`, and `notes` for these service records. It does not
   expose contact details, hours, steps, or structured requirements for
   editing. Do not assume those fields can be corrected in this UI. If a
   factual correction to a non-editable field is needed, stop and ask the
   project maintainer to handle it through the approved maintenance process;
   do not bypass the workflow.
5. To submit a verification request, use the record's **Request
   verification** form. Its source URL is read-only and comes from the record;
   the URL must be HTTPS on the configured exact official-host allowlist
   (`davaocity.gov.ph`, `www.davaocity.gov.ph`, `hrmo.davaocity.gov.ph`,
   `rsso11.psa.gov.ph`, `psa.gov.ph`, or `comelec.gov.ph`). Enter the actual
   source access date (not a future date) and a comparison note of 10–2,000
   characters identifying fields checked and unresolved gaps. The UI/API
   currently accepts one primary source URL; note additional evidence sources

6. The request is sent to the existing local workflow route

### Reviewer 2 — independent decision

1. A different person must sign in with their own `reviewer` or `admin`
   account, open `/admin/review`, and independently inspect the request's
   source, access date, comparison note, and current record. The queue card
   does not show a field-by-field diff; navigate through Records to the
   applicable record and check retained evidence directly.
2. Approve only after independently confirming that all publishable factual
   fields are supported and that unresolved information remains withheld or
   explicitly unconfirmed. Reject if evidence is insufficient or corrections
   are needed, and document the next steps through the existing maintenance
   process. The UI decision calls
   `POST /api/review/:requestId/decision`; `reviewer` and `admin` roles are
   permitted, while server-side checks enforce the role.
3. The submitter's UI disables both decision buttons. The server-side decision
   logic also rejects either approval or rejection when the authenticated
   account submitted the request; a caller-supplied account ID is not used.
   Only an approved request sets a record to verified.

### Identity limitation and operational control

The server enforces submitter-versus-decider separation by account ID for both
decision outcomes. It does not prove that different accounts belong to
different real-world people and cannot prevent one person from controlling
multiple accounts. An admin/coordinator must designate two distinct people
outside the app, ensure they use their own accounts, and retain an operational
reviewer assignment/decision record alongside the evidence. Do not describe
account-ID separation as identity verification.

Until both real reviewers independently complete the existing approval
process, all three final statuses remain **PENDING**, catalog verification
must remain `unverified`, and `lastVerified` must remain `null`. Evidence
statuses in this queue are not workflow decisions.
